How surfaces are classified

Roofs, conventional concrete/asphalt, and covered decks/porches count at 100%. Approved permeable systems may be excluded for this planning estimate only after you confirm the Town approved their pervious treatment. Ordinary gravel, uncovered slatted decks, and pool basin/water area remain unresolved until the Town confirms their treatment. No automatic partial-credit rates are assumed. Concrete pool surrounds count as hard surfaces.

Sources: Ordinance 105 definitions, §5.4.3.1, Figure 7.2 and §7.8; Ordinance 87C definitions and Section B.1. Approval of a product alone does not establish a coverage credit.
